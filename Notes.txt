# Rate Options
Baud	Bits/sec	Bytes/sec	1 sec burst
56	56	~7 B/s	useless for data
1200	1200	~150 B/s	small payload
2400	2400	~300 B/s	usable burst     * Default!!!
4800	4800	~600 B/s	fast audio modem
9600	9600	~1.2 KB/s	unstable but fast



🧠 What you now have
✔ Framing system
START::  [payload]  ::END
✔ Basic error tolerance
sender repeats packet twice
receiver naturally ignores duplicates due to same payload
✔ File-safe transport
base64 ensures binary-safe transmission

mini audio packet network layer
